# Unalakleet Airport (PAUN)

Elevate your flight simulation experience with our meticulously crafted scenery add-on for PAUN Unalakleet Airport. This add-on brings the airport to life, featuring a variety of models that capture the essence of this unique Alaskan hub.

Unalakleet (YOO-nə-lə-kleet) is a city in Nome Census Area, Alaska, United States, in the western part of the state. At the 2010 census the population was 765, up from 688 in 2000. Unalakleet is known in the region and around Alaska for its salmon and king crab harvests; the residents rely for much of their diet on caribou, ptarmigan, oogruk (bearded seal), and various salmon species.
